    Your answers will show up here.

    I built MathBot to practise mental math the way it actually comes up in trading and quant interviews: someone says the question out loud and you answer on the spot. Most practice sites only show the question on screen, which is a different skill. Here the question is spoken, you type the answer, and you get +1 for a right answer and −1 for a wrong one.

    By default, addition and subtraction use numbers from 2 to 100, and multiplication is (2 to 12) × (2 to 100). You can raise the ranges in Settings. If you can do 4-digit by 4-digit multiplication in your head, you can legally call yourself a human calculator.
